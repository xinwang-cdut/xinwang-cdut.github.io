Today, the laboratory held the <a href="https://www.simr2023.org/">SIMR</a> Conference. During the meeting, our team had in-depth discussions and thorough exchanges with several internationally renowned experts on cascade hazards. 

---

#### Meeting Contents

<ul>
    <li> Prof. Xin Wang shared our recent progress on global investigation of cascading hazards.</li>
    <li> Prof. John Jansen and Prof. Yu Deng introduced our latest work on mass balance and transport in Sedongpu Valley.</li>
    <li> Prof. Xuanmei Fan systematically introduced the work we had carried out and encourage everyone to have a discussion. The valuable ideas and suggestions put forward by the participant experts on the spot. !</li>
</ul>
